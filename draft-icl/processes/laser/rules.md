# Laser

- Inspect cutting results: hole/slot sizes, flat hole positions, cut profile/blank size and specified burr/dross/material conditions.
- Default tolerance: ±0.2 mm (see SKILL.md defaults). Do not apply it to stock thickness or visual criteria.
- Use the supplied approved flat pattern/cutting drawing or reference blank. Prefer one large horizontal flat reference. When only formed STEP is supplied, develop a reference only with exact CAD geometry and established bend allowances, or an explicitly user-authorized estimate. An estimated reference is not an approved production blank: retain assumptions and verification of every outer/cutout contour, and keep blank/position acceptance TBC until the approved flat/program exists. Do not promote an example K-factor into a default. A formed STEP alone does not establish blank dimensions, bend allowance or flat hole positions. If the flat source is missing, request it or keep those checks TBC; identify any formed reference view honestly.
- Do not add formed flange heights or assembly distances. Keep IDs, dimensions, datum and gauge contacts consistent.
- Preserve start-of-run and hourly groups. Do not merge inspection frequencies or invent technology/tip/material settings.
- Keep unavailable flat pattern/program approvals in the short To confirm items and full spec evidence; include an operator flat-position/profile check only with an approved drawing and a practical specified method.
- Use Vernier for accessible stock thickness and cut dimensions. A pin gauge or other tool needs confirmed operator availability. The included company profile appends one final hourly check: `Ensure no harmful burrs, sharp edges, laser daggs etc | SOP008 | Visual`. Keep the SOP reference; its detailed criteria come from the company's controlled SOP, not CAD. A matching existing quality row keeps its stable key and is not duplicated.
- Group matching holes under one checkpoint and one common balloon. Point its square leaders to 2–3 representative holes when available, using `drawing.balloons[].targets`; one hole needs one target. Group only features with the same size, acceptance limits, gauge and inspection frequency. Retain the actual feature references and inspection coverage in the method; illustrative leaders do not change the required sampling.
- A slot width and length described in one cell use one checkpoint and one balloon, not separate width/length IDs. Store the two measured values in the check's `measurements`, and use dimension `measurement` keys for the width/length graphics. Set those detail dimensions' `show_badge: false` so they do not duplicate the common label. Keep both numeric dimension values visible if needed, and record both sets of gauge contacts in the method. Separate checkpoints remain necessary when acceptance limits, gauges or frequencies differ.

Use `template.xlsx` and `layout.json`. Geometry-specific sample hole sizes are not defaults.

Show concise hole/slot sizes next to their shared balloons on the complete reference. Keep numeric feature details only when needed for a practical contact or location check. Include the common reference-sample row before hourly cut-feature checks; keep SOP008 last. Inspect print-size readability with the included doubled Laser balloon scale.
