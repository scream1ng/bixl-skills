# Welding

- Require assembly CAD/drawings identifying parts A/B, joints and specified weld requirements. Do not infer an assembly from an unidentified single solid.
- Inspect relative orientation/position, functional flange-to-flange or hole-centre assembly distances, distortion and specified weld placement/size/length.
- Do not repeat upstream hole diameters/blank sizes. Centre-to-centre assembly checks need an actual fixture/gauge or documented derivation, not a claimed direct vernier contact.
- Default tolerance: ±0.5 mm, clinch-fastener holes ±0.2 mm (see SKILL.md defaults). Weld, angular/geometric and visual acceptance require their own criteria.
- Verify gauge access after joining; fixture location is separate from final acceptance.

No company welding form has been supplied. Preview a draft, but obtain the actual form and inspected layout mapping before Excel. Never silently borrow the Laser/Bending template.
